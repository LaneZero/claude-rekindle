# ClaudeRekindle v2.7.2-beta.1: Local reset countdown

- Detects `You are out of free messages until 10:00 PM` in Claude composer banners in addition to pause cards and near-limit advisories.
- Stores a full local timestamp (milliseconds since epoch) per conversation. Dates/times in the popup use the browser/device local timezone.
- Shows remaining time with the local reset clock in the page panel and browser popup. The popup recalculates continuously while open.
- After the announced time, reports **availability not confirmed**, never falsely claiming that the account quota has reset.
- A quota banner without an enabled Continue never triggers a click. Existing protections remain.
- Tampermonkey detects the same quota message and displays a local countdown in its page panel. Its implementation remains separate from the extension core.

## Caveat
Claude's displayed clock has no explicit timezone or date. It is interpreted as local browser time, as requested. A conflicting Claude timezone setting, stale page, manual system-clock change, or stale warning may produce an inaccurate estimate. Only the actual enabled Continue button is actionable.

## Manual QA
1. Open a chat with `You are out of free messages until ...` and no pause card. Confirm the panel shows local time and remaining hours/minutes without clicking anything.
2. Confirm popup shows the same remaining time. Leave the popup open until the minute changes.
3. Test a limit crossing midnight; restore the tab and confirm the old saved reset does not automatically become tomorrow.
4. After the reset time, check for `availability not confirmed` until Claude actually enables Continue.
5. Check Firefox Desktop, Chrome, Firefox Android separately. Android background timers are not guaranteed.
